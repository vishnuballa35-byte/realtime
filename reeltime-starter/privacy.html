Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

VN ("we", "us") is a social app for sharing short videos and chatting. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using VN you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and username. Your password is handled by our login provider (Supabase Auth); we never see or store it ourselves.
  • Profile: your profile photo and bio, if you add them.
  • Content you create: Reels (videos) and captions, comments, likes, follows, and Reel views.
  • Messages and media: the messages, photos and videos you send in chats.
  • Calls: a record of who called whom and when. Audio and video travel between the devices on the call. We do not record calls.
  • Device and technical data: your browser stores some data on your device (such as saved settings and cached app files) so the app works quickly and can be installed. Our hosting and database providers may log technical data such as IP address and browser type to keep the service running and secure.

2. Camera, microphone and notifications

VN asks for permission to use your camera and microphone only for calls, and to access your photos and videos when you choose to upload or send them. You can turn these permissions off in your browser or phone settings at any time; calls will not work without them.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account, log you in and show your profile.
  • To deliver your messages, calls, Reels, comments and notifications.
  • To keep VN safe: preventing spam and abuse, handling reports and blocks.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising based on your private messages.

4. What other people can see

Your display name, username, profile photo, bio, Reels, likes and comments can be seen by other logged-in users. Your messages and chat media can be seen only by you and the person you are chatting with. Anyone you chat with can save or screenshot what you send, so share carefully.

5. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run VN: Supabase (login, database and file storage) and Netlify (website hosting). They process data on our behalf and only to provide their services. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of users or the public.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete a message, a Reel or a chat, we remove it from our systems. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile and content, except where we must keep something for legal or safety reasons.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name, username, photo and bio from your profile.
  • Delete your messages, chats and Reels inside the app.
  • Block other users.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account, by emailing us at the address below. We will reply within a reasonable time.

8. Children

VN is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, please email us and we will remove it.

9. Security

We use access rules so that private data such as messages can be read only by the people involved, and we use encrypted connections (HTTPS). No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device safe.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app.

11. Contact us
